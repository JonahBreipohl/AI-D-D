# Risk register

Status: **Active Phase 0 register; review at every gate**

Scores are pre-control estimates: likelihood (L) 1–5 × impact (I) 1–5. Critical is 20–25, high is 15–19, medium is 8–14. Owners are accountable for evidence and escalation, not necessarily all mitigation work.

## Risk review rules

- Critical risks are reviewed at least at every milestone and whenever an indicator fires.
- A risk without a named owner, measurable indicator, and contingency is not “accepted”; it is unmanaged.
- Residual scores are assigned only after mitigation evidence exists.
- Qualified counsel, not A7 or A0, is required for commercial branding, final notices/terms, user-import features, public user-generated content, and jurisdiction-specific privacy questions.
- The owner may accept a residual business risk, but cannot waive a license term, security fact, or failed correctness test by relabeling it low priority.
