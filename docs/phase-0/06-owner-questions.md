# Questions for the owner

Status: **Answered 2026-09-17 — the owner approved all recommended defaults without exceptions.**

The questions are retained below as the audit trail for the accepted decision batch. Implementation remains subject to the separate G0 gate.

1. **Rules and slice boundary:** Will you approve the recommended Phase 2 proof—one 60–90 minute original scenario, one level-3 pre-generated character, social + exploration + one combat encounter, success/failure/recovery endings, and only the explicitly declared mechanics—rather than attempting a broadly complete fifth-edition engine first?

2. **Rules edition:** Should v1 pin **SRD 5.2.1 / revised 2024 rules under CC BY 4.0** as recommended, or is compatibility with the 2014 rules important enough to choose the CC edition of SRD 5.1 instead?

3. **Adventure source:** Will you approve a wholly original SRD-safe adventure for v1 and defer both published-module import and third-party adventures as recommended?

4. **Fairness, correction, and persona:** Will you approve no secret fudging; pre-roll commitment of rulings/DCs/modifiers/targets/consequences; visible mercy only; the recommended consent model (visible automatic compensation for invalid system events, host-proposed discretionary rewind approved by every directly affected player with no inferred consent from absent players, solo-player approval in Phase 2, and unilateral safety pause by anyone); and the single Phase 2 **Transparent Guide** persona (concise, beginner-friendly, neutral rules strictness, medium declared threat, no hidden mercy, explanations on request)?

5. **Content safety:** Will you approve an adults-only but Teen/PG-13 content ceiling for v1—ordinary fantasy violence and mild horror allowed; explicit sexual content, sexual violence, hate-based targeting, self-harm encouragement, and graphic torture/gore excluded—plus pre-session lines/veils/phobias and an out-of-band pause/stop/report control that bypasses the model?

6. **Turn choreography and economics:** Will you approve the tiered-hybrid hypothesis—bounded intent/proposal first, authoritative validation/roll/apply second, outcome narration only afterward, deterministic result templates as timeout/budget fallback—and require the p50-under-$1, p95-under-$2 per three-hour session plus p95 latency targets to be measured across the entire multi-call logical turn before they become accepted gates?

7. **Retention, export, and deletion:** Will you approve local-first Phase 2 records—events and exact narration remain on the tester’s device until export or deletion; centralized telemetry is metadata-only and retained for 30 days; no raw prompts/backstories/role-play are centrally logged—and defer the hosted game-record retention/deletion SLA to a Phase 3 legal/privacy gate?

8. **Product intent:** Is the goal a commercial-capable product that begins as a private unpaid alpha, as recommended, or strictly a hobby tool for you and friends? In what country/state will the operator and initial alpha users be based?

9. **Model/provider policy:** Will you approve a centrally funded commercial API for Phase 2 behind a provider-neutral adapter, with a game-specific model bake-off and no raw role-play in operational telemetry? Do you have any required provider, prohibited provider, data region, zero-retention, or monthly-spend constraint?

10. **Authority and scale:** For eventual remote multiplayer, should the service be the neutral authority as recommended, or should a player’s machine/self-hosted server remain authoritative despite the weaker anti-cheat and reconnect guarantees? Is an invite-only alpha target of 10 simultaneous tables / 50 connected users sufficient?

11. **Audience and age:** Will you approve an adults-only, invite-only initial alpha with no public signup, and defer any support for minors until a separate privacy/safety/legal gate?

12. **Spatial experience:** Will you approve theater of the mind with explicit encounter zones/range bands, while excluding a tactical grid, map editor, line of sight, and precise movement renderer from v1?

13. **Branding:** Will you approve using an original public product name and treating “AI Dungeon Master for D&D 5e” as an internal description only, with any public compatibility wording and use of “Dungeon Master” subject to qualified trademark review?

14. **AI party members:** Will you confirm that AI-controlled player characters remain out through Phase 4 and can only be reconsidered at the Phase 5 gate?

15. **Voice:** Will you confirm text-only v1, with keyboard and screen-reader accessibility, and defer speech input/output?

16. **Phase 1 model spike:** May A2/A6/A8 run a short, disposable model feasibility test against mocks in parallel with the deterministic Phase 1 engine—unable to touch authoritative state—or do you want the original “no AI whatsoever until Phase 2” boundary kept literally, which makes the equivalent Phase 2F feasibility gate mandatory before integration?

17. **Success gates:** Subject to the full-turn measurement in question 6, do you approve the charter’s proposed completion, replay-intent, fun/trust, correctness, zero-visibility-leak, content-safety/stop, latency, and cost thresholds as the precommitted Phase 2 go/change/stop criteria?
