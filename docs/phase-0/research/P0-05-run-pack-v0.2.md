## 6. Out-of-band controls

The three controls must be separately visible, keyboard- and screen-reader-equivalent, and independent of the Wizard.

### PAUSE

> **PAUSE** — Stop narration, dice, timers, and state now. I do not need to give a reason. Nothing resumes unless I explicitly choose to resume and agree on a safe restart point. I may instead choose a break, an accepted skip/fade/rewind, or to end.

Stop immediately, touch no dice/state, and ask only which listed option the participant wants. Never infer resume consent.

### STOP

> **STOP** — End play now. Stop narration, dice, timers, and state. I do not need to give a reason, and my incentive is unaffected.

Stop immediately. Do not convert STOP into PAUSE or request justification.

### REPORT

> **REPORT** — Open a separate human route for a product, fairness, privacy, safety, or research-conduct concern. During active play, reporting pauses narration, dice, timers, and state before anything is collected. I may provide an optional category and optional text. After acknowledgement, I choose whether to resume or stop. A post-session report does not change completed game state.

Only coded session/turn ID attaches by default—never transcript, backstory, contact data, boundary detail, or full session. Recipient, access, acknowledgement, retention/deletion, follow-up, token route, and any mandatory escalation disclosure are open preflight blockers. REPORT is not an emergency or clinical service.

Practice PAUSE or STOP on neutral content and separately walk through a dummy REPORT. No fake incident or real concern detail is created.

## 7. Guide and operating discipline

- Be concise, beginner-friendly, neutral in strictness, and explicit that threat is medium.
- Label each resolution `deterministic`, bounded project ruling `AM01-R1`, or `unsupported/clarify`. There is no generic bounded-ruling lane.
- Offer examples as optional, not a closed menu unless the rules truly permit only those choices.
- Ask one neutral clarification only when actor, target, rule, cost, visibility, or consequence would differ.
- Write and expose the complete commitment before touching dice; apply state and append the event before narration.
- Never coach toward an asset, route, continued combat, or preferred ending; never secretly rescue or tune difficulty.
- Immediately yield to any control, boundary, consent, privacy, or safety halt.
- If a card, reviewed fact, baseline value, or exact state transition is absent, stop. Do not improvise.

## 8. Exact resolution flow

For every consequential action:

1. Restate the player's goal neutrally and clarify once if needed.
2. Verify control state, actor, phase, target, visibility, range/engagement, hands, resources, and accepted-envelope membership.
3. Select an exact entry in the [operator baseline v0.2](../evidence/P0-06-provisional-operator-baseline-v0.2.md).
4. Record and expose: action/check/attack ID; actor/target; rule or `AM01-R1`; DC/AC; modifier; Advantage/Disadvantage; resource/item cost; success/hit/miss/critical possibilities; exact state consequence; visibility; pre-state/version.
5. Roll visible dice only after the participant acknowledges the commitment. The participant initiates every Rin-owned roll; the operator initiates every Watcher-owned roll. Record actor, roller, and raw dice in order. Heroic Inspiration can reroll only a Rin-owned die the participant just rolled.
6. Resolve legal replacement/after-failure resources at their stated timing; never change the original commitment.
7. Apply authoritative state, mark terminal state if any, and append an immutable event.
8. Narrate only the applied delta. A dispute pauses dependent resolution.

An invalid event gets a visible correction/fork. A valid unfavorable roll is not error. A discretionary rewind requires the solo player's explicit consent, names the checkpoint, preserves the abandoned branch, and never selects a preferred reroll.

- Watcher Initiative is static 12 and never rolled; Rin rolls at Advantage +2 and wins a total-12 tie.
- Watcher uses the deterministic stand/move/engage/Multiattack/Dash/Ready-re-evaluation policy. It targets only Rin, never retreats/escapes, takes the first legal OA, and uses Str +2 against Grapple/Shove.
- Track movement spent and Dash actions under the operator formula; recompute from current Speed after Slow or Grapple release without undoing movement. A Watcher that Dashes, spends 20 ft., then becomes Slowed has 10 ft. remaining.
- `AM01-R1`: Watcher OA is one Slam, never Multiattack. Slam damage is visible `1d6+2`, critical `2d6+2`; static 5 is unused.
- Both actors begin with Reaction available. Rin may choose a nonlethal knockout when a qualifying melee final blow would reduce the Watcher to 0; a ranged thrown Javelin cannot do so. Either Watcher defeat path is combat victory.
- Ready spends Rin's action, fixes one visible state/event trigger plus an admitted action or movement payload, uses and competes for Rin's Reaction after the trigger finishes, may be ignored, and expires before Rin's next turn. During the Watcher's turn, re-evaluate it after every authoritative mid-turn state change, not only a Ready payload; off-turn changes never execute Watcher policy and are first considered at its next turn start.
- Rin may release a Grapple at any time with no action at an atomic event boundary. Release frees the hand, restores current Speed, and recomputes movement. During the Watcher's turn, if release occurs inside Ready/OA/attack/movement, finish that enclosing payload before Watcher policy re-evaluation; off turn, record the change without invoking Watcher policy. Terminal state still halts immediately. Never restore spent economy.
- Same-zone states are engaged 5 ft. / disengaged 10 ft.; adjacent edges 20 ft.; entering occupied zone is disengaged until a 5-ft. engagement step.
- Item uses, hands, thrown locations, conditions, resources, and unsupported actions follow the exact operator baseline.
- Mandatory nonlethal order replaces a would-be 0-HP result with 1 HP; atomically applies the full Unconscious state (including Incapacitated, Prone, Speed 0, and dropped held items); begins but does not complete a Short Rest; then immediately ends combat as failure.

Retreat requires lawful crossing of the boundary 20 ft. beyond Entry Deck, including disengagement and any OA. Knockout on the final OA outranks retreat. The Watcher never pursues. `exactly_one_combat_played=yes` only after both actors begin a turn or an earlier valid combat sequence becomes terminal. A first-turn boundary retreat before that predicate is an in-fiction failure but not a qualifying completion. Never coach the player to remain.

## 15. Rescue and disposition

- Rescue: nonstandard staff help that enables progress outside the frozen pack.
- Structural softlock: no admitted transition can reach an ending without changing the contract.
- Usability softlock: route exists but participant cannot identify/execute it after three minutes and one neutral reorientation: “Please tell me your current goal and what information or action you think is available.”
- Near-softlock: recovery takes two failed good-faith attempts, more than two minutes of confusion, or repeated materially wrong state understanding.

Approved rule explanation, repeated public fact, accommodation, unsupported-action script, and safety procedure are not rescues. When uncertain, classify as rescue. Retain all consent-permitted non-completions and contrary evidence; never relabel them to improve the denominator.
