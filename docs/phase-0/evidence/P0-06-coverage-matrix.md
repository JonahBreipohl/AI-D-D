## Purpose and completion boundary

This matrix narrows the pinned SRD to the rules and content needed for *The Signal at Glasswater Weir*. It traces every mechanic expressly admitted by P0-03, identifies the mechanics that are outside the slice, and assigns a direct test obligation. It does not authorize implementation, broaden the allowlist, resolve a P0-03 contract gap, or substitute a document for player evidence.

P0-06 can be marked complete only after all of the following are true:

1. P0-05 has produced its required evidence and every accepted slice revision has been incorporated into a versioned matrix.
2. Blockers `B-01` through `B-11` have written resolutions accepted by the human owner and recorded through A0. **Satisfied 2026-09-23 by Amendment 01; future contract changes still require A0 plus human-owner acceptance.**
3. Every `IN` row has a stable coverage ID, a named downstream contract surface, and a concrete test obligation ready for P0-12 and P0-13 to consume, with no unsupported mechanic accepted by default. P0-06 does not depend on completion of those downstream tasks.
4. A1 and A7 confirm that every rules-bearing field has the pinned source locator and `SRD_5_2_1_CC_BY_4_0` provenance, while project-original content remains separately classified.

## Locator and status conventions

All page numbers below are **1-based physical PDF pages**, not viewer labels inferred from another edition. The cited entries were checked against the local pinned file at `third_party/srd/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf` by extracting and reviewing the relevant pages: 5-18, 20-24, 47-49, 83, 86-100, 176-191, 202-203, and 259. No rule locator was taken from a different SRD, a rulebook, or a web summary.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `IN` | Deterministic mechanic or fixed data is in the accepted slice. |
| `IN-ORIGINAL` | Project-authored state/content is in the slice but has no SRD rule locator; it must not be relabeled SRD-derived. |
| `BLOCKED` | Reserved for a newly discovered contract gap. No current row remains blocked by `B-01` through `B-11`. |
| `OUT` | Explicitly unsupported; input must be rejected or clarified without consuming an action, resource, or state. |
| `SPEC_ONLY` | The requirement exists only in documents/prototype materials. No production implementation is claimed. |

Unless a row says otherwise, implementation status is `SPEC_ONLY / NOT IMPLEMENTED`, and evidence status is `P0-05 NOT OBSERVED`.
