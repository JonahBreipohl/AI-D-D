## Accepted amendment

The owner accepted the narrow baseline below for P0-05/P0-06 research on 2026-09-23. It resolves the contract decisions for provisional blockers `B-01` through `B-11`, removes an unused mechanic, and makes the admitted scenario reproducible without adding a grid, hazard, enemy, item benefit, or live facilitator choice. The accepted [base P0-03 specification](P0-03-vertical-slice-spec.md) plus this amendment is the active research contract; this amendment controls wherever the two conflict. Acceptance does **not** complete P0-06, clear the P0-04/P0-05 fielding preflight, authorize recruitment or human research, open G0, or authorize production.

## Rationale and downstream effect

This is the smallest fair baseline because it narrows unsupported surface area, exposes every consequential choice before dice, preserves Rin, the Watcher stat block, the scene graph, exactly one combat, and all accepted endings, and makes identical state produce identical operator behavior. Its main tradeoff is deliberately artificial, predictable zone combat; P0-05 should measure whether that harms agency or trust rather than letting a facilitator repair it live.

Owner acceptance resolves the P0-03 contract decisions for `B-01` through `B-11`. The provisional P0-06 matrix, operator baseline v0.2, and P0-05 run pack v0.2 now incorporate those decisions; required human review, version-matched dry-run evidence, and matching P0-12 contract/P0-13 test work remain open. Human-research/privacy/safety/accessibility preflight, scene/provenance review, and all other fielding blockers remain open. P0-06 remains provisional until its stated evidence and reviews are complete.
